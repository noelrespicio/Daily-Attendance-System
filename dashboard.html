Quick guide

How to Use Attendance Mode

Follow these steps before recording your attendance.

1

Automatic Mode — Recommended

Keep the switch OFF and check that the label says AUTO. Press your desired attendance button. The system uses the current time from your device.

2

Manual Mode — Choose Your Time

Turn the switch ON until the label says MANUAL. Select the hour, minute, and second. Check the preview, then press the correct attendance button.

3

Confirm or Cancel

A 5-second countdown appears after pressing a button. Press Cancel if you made a mistake. Otherwise, wait for the system to save the entry.

4

Switch Back to Automatic

Turn the switch OFF again. The label returns to AUTO, and the system uses the current device time for your next attendance entry.

Important: Manual mode changes the recorded time, not the attendance date. Time In and Time Out still require location permission. Use manual time only when authorized.
