About the platform

One place for your daily attendance

The Daily Time Attendance System is a web-based tool intended to make employee attendance easier to record and review. Digital records can reduce reliance on paper logs and help employees keep a clearer picture of their workday.

Everyday employee benefits

Designed to make work routines easier

A consistent attendance routine helps employees stay organized throughout the day. The system supports record keeping; it does not replace company rules or supervisor verification.

  • ✓Saves time: enter attendance digitally instead of relying only on handwritten logs.
  • ✓Improves organization: keep time-in, break, and time-out details together.
  • ✓Helps self-checking: review saved entries and notice missing records sooner.
  • ✓Supports daily planning: follow a clearer routine for starting work and taking breaks.
  • ✓Convenient access: use a compatible phone, tablet, or computer with internet access.
Getting started

How to use the system

Follow your company's attendance policies and use the options available in your account.

STEP 01

Create an account

Fill in your name, email, employee ID, branch, and password.

STEP 02

Sign in

Use your registered email and password to access your dashboard.

STEP 03

Record attendance

Select the correct attendance action and allow permissions if required.

STEP 04

Review records

Check saved entries and report errors using your workplace process.

Privacy and responsible use

Attendance records may contain personal work information. Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared device.

  • Enter manual times only when your workplace permits it.
  • Check the date and attendance action before saving.
  • GPS or camera functions require compatible hardware and browser permissions.
  • Available features depend on the deployed dashboard and organization settings.
