About the platform

What is the Employee Attendance System?

It is a web-based tool that helps organize employee attendance records digitally. Instead of relying only on handwritten notes or scattered records, employees can use one dashboard to record and review important time entries.

🟢

Time In and Time Out

Record the beginning and end of a workday. When enabled by the system, automatic attendance can use the device's current time and request location access for selected actions.

☕

Lunch and Coffee Breaks

Record Lunch Break Out/In and Coffee Break Out/In to help keep daily work and break entries clear and consistent.

🔁

Automatic and Manual Modes

If the deployed dashboard includes the mode switch, employees can use the normal time-recording flow or select a time manually when authorized to do so.

📋

Attendance History

Review recorded dates and time entries to help check personal attendance details and identify missing entries that may need attention.

📍

Location Support

For attendance actions configured to use GPS, the browser or device may ask permission to access location. Location accuracy depends on the device and its surroundings.

📦

Work Tools in One Place

The broader app may also provide links to sellout or inventory records, helping staff access related work tools without searching through many pages.

Everyday benefits

How can it help employees in daily life?

A clear attendance routine can make the workday easier to manage. The system is intended to support better organization—not to replace company policies or supervisor verification.

  • ✓ Saves time: record attendance digitally instead of depending only on handwritten logs.
  • ✓ Improves organization: keep time-in, breaks, and time-out entries together.
  • ✓ Supports self-checking: review records and notice missing or incorrect entries earlier.
  • ✓ Helps daily planning: make it easier to remember work start, break, and end-of-day routines.
  • ✓ Reduces paper handling: digital records can reduce reliance on paper logs, depending on workplace procedures.
  • ✓ Convenient access: use a compatible phone, tablet, or computer with an internet connection.
Getting started

STEP 02

Open attendance

STEP 03

Record your time

Select the correct attendance action. Allow location access if the selected action requires it.

STEP 04

Review your entries

Check your saved time records and report discrepancies through your workplace's normal process.

Privacy, accuracy, and responsible use

Attendance records may contain personal work information. Keep your password private, sign out on shared devices, and grant location or camera permissions only when needed for an attendance function.

  • Only enter manual times when your workplace permits it.
  • Check the date and selected action before saving an entry.
  • GPS and camera functions require compatible hardware, browser permissions, and a supported connection.
  • Availability of features depends on the version deployed by your organization and its database settings.
